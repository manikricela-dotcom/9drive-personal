Privacy Policy for 9Drive Personal

9Drive Personal is a private, self-hosted application operated solely by its owner for personal storage management.

Information Accessed

When authorized via Google OAuth, 9Drive Personal accesses Google Drive storage quota metadata and manages files exclusively within the owner's dedicated storage folder.

Data Storage & Sharing

OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted and stored solely on the owner's private server database. No user data, file contents, or credentials are shared with, sold to, or transmitted to any third party.
